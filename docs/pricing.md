# Pricing

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Who it's for |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 forever | Individual users — full P2P sharing, @handle, encryption |
| **Pro** | $9/mo | Power users — unlimited pals, relay quota, advanced tools |
| **Team** | $12/seat/mo | Organizations — SSO, admin dashboard, compliance |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Large deployments — SLA, self-hosted discovery, dedicated support |

## What's Included

### Free
- Full P2P encrypted file sharing (ChaCha20-Poly1305)
- @handle identity
- E2E encrypted chat
- Up to 10 pals
- 5 GB relay quota
- Share links
- Activity log
- Email & OAuth recovery
- All free extensions

### Pro ($9/mo)
Everything in Free, plus:
- Unlimited pals
- 50 GB relay quota
- Priority TURN relay
- VFS (virtual drive)
- File manager context menu
- Transfer analytics
- Media streaming
- Custom themes

### Team ($12/seat/mo)
Everything in Pro, plus:
- Organization & department management
- Admin dashboard
- SSO / SAML 2.0 / OIDC
- Audit log export
- Webhooks
- SCIM provisioning
- Compliance controls

### Enterprise (Custom)
Everything in Team, plus:
- SLA guarantee
- Self-hosted discovery server
- Dedicated support
- Custom contract & invoicing
- Private overlay networks

## Extension Tiers

Extensions are gated by subscription plan:

| Tier | Requires | Examples |
|------|----------|---------|
| Free | Any plan | sync, chat, share-links, groups, discovery, audit |
| Pro | Pro or above | vfs, explorer-integration, analytics, media-streaming |
| Enterprise | Team/Enterprise | networks, auth-sso, turn-relay |

## Billing

- Billed via Lemon Squeezy (credit card, PayPal)
- Monthly billing — cancel any time
- Pro features are disabled (not deleted) when subscription ends
- Annual plan: contact hello@palexplorer.com
